**Figure 7. Consensus method-sensitive salivary RNA signature across extracellular-vesicle isolation procedures.**

Each point represents one of 97 consensus features supported by at least one robust paired contrast. The x-axis shows the cumulative absolute effect across robust contrasts, while the y-axis shows the number of robust paired contrasts. Point size represents the maximum absolute paired effect. Marker shape denotes the conservative name-based feature class. Labels identify selected high-ranking or otherwise informative features: MIR29B1, MIR193B, MIR885, MIR136, MIR32, MIR1249. MIR1249 was the highest-ranked feature, supported by 6 robust contrasts and a cumulative robust effect of 20.29. The three-donor analysis is exploratory and describes isolation-method sensitivity rather than formal differential expression.
